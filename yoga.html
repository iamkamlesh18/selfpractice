Surya Namaskar — the sun salutation — is twelve linked poses that carry you through one complete round of breath. Below is the full sequence with Sanskrit names, the mantra for each step, and what each pose asks of the body.

The sun salutation

Twelve steps, one breath

Move on the breath: inhale to lengthen, exhale to fold and release, and hold the breath briefly at the eight-limbed salute. Two sets of twelve poses — right leg leading, then left — make one complete round.

Inhale — lengthen, lift, open
Exhale — fold, lower, release
Hold — stay still, stay soft
01

Prayer Pose

Pranamasana

Exhale Anahata (heart)

Stand at the front of the mat, feet together. Bring the palms together at the heart, shoulders soft, eyes gently closed. Exhale as you arrive.

Mantra Om Mitraya Namah Salutations to the friend of all

Balances the body and relaxes the nervous system.

02

Raised Arms

Hasta Uttanasana

Inhale Vishuddha (throat)

Inhale and sweep the arms overhead, biceps beside the ears. Reach up from the heels to the fingertips; a small, kind backbend is plenty.

Mantra Om Ravaye Namah Salutations to the shining, radiant one

Stretches and tones the abdomen and expands the chest for a fuller breath.

03

Standing Forward Fold

Padahastasana

Exhale Svadhisthana (sacral)

Exhale and hinge from the hips, folding forward with a long spine. Keep the knees soft and place the palms beside the feet.

Mantra Om Suryaya Namah Salutations to the sun, dispeller of darkness

Makes the waist and spine flexible and stretches the hamstrings.

04

Equestrian Pose

Ashwa Sanchalanasana

Inhale Ajna (third eye)

Inhale and take the right leg back, right knee to the floor. The left foot stays between the palms; press the hips forward and look up.

Mantra Om Bhanave Namah Salutations to the one who illuminates

Strengthens the legs and makes the spine and neck flexible.

05

Plank / Mountain

Dandasana / Parvatasana

Exhale Vishuddha (throat)

Exhale and take the left leg back so the body forms one long line. Arms under the shoulders, hips level, gaze down and slightly forward.

Mantra Om Khagaya Namah Salutations to the one who moves through the sky

Strengthens the arms and back and improves posture.

06

Eight-Limbed Salute

Ashtanga Namaskara

Hold (breath suspended) Manipura (solar plexus)

Gently lower the knees, chest and chin to the floor — eight points of contact. Keep the hips slightly lifted and the elbows tucked.

Mantra Om Pushne Namah Salutations to the nourisher of all

Strengthens the chest and arms and gently tones the abdomen.

07

Cobra

Bhujangasana

Inhale Svadhisthana (sacral)

Inhale and slide forward, pressing the palms down to lift the chest. Elbows soft, shoulders away from the ears, gaze forward.

Mantra Om Hiranyagarbhaya Namah Salutations to the golden cosmic self

Strengthens the spine, opens the lungs and relieves stiffness.

08

Downward Dog

Adho Mukha Svanasana

Exhale Vishuddha (throat)

Exhale and lift the hips high and back into an inverted V. Heels reach for the floor, head relaxed between the arms.

Mantra Om Marichaye Namah Salutations to the one with infinite rays

Strengthens arms and legs and increases blood flow to the spine.

09

Equestrian Pose

Ashwa Sanchalanasana

Inhale Ajna (third eye)

Inhale and step the right foot forward between the palms, left knee down. This mirrors step four.

Mantra Om Adityaya Namah Salutations to the infinite, son of Aditi

Stretches the hips and strengthens the legs.

10

Standing Forward Fold

Padahastasana

Exhale Svadhisthana (sacral)

Exhale and step the left foot forward to meet the right. Fold forward once more, palms beside the feet.

Mantra Om Savitre Namah Salutations to the one responsible for life

Stretches the hamstrings and opens the hips, shoulders and arms.

11

Raised Arms

Hasta Uttanasana

Inhale Vishuddha (throat)

Inhale and roll the spine up, sweeping the arms overhead. Reach up more than you bend back.

Mantra Om Arkaya Namah Salutations to the one worthy of praise and glory

Expands the chest and tones the abdominal muscles.

12

Prayer Pose

Pranamasana

Exhale Anahata (heart)

Exhale and return the hands to the heart. Stand still for one full breath — one round is complete.

Mantra Om Bhaskaraya Namah Salutations to the light that illumines

Grounds the body and settles the breath before the next round.

Build up slowly 3 · 6 · 12 rounds over several weeks. Practise on an empty stomach, at least two hours after a meal, and never strain a joint to reach a shape.

Chant with the pose

The twelve mantras

Supportive movement

Simple exercises

Short mobility work that prepares the body for practice — and undoes a day at a desk.

Neck & shoulder rolls

2 min · gentle

Slow half-circles with the chin, then ten shoulder rolls each way. Release the neck before any backbend.

Cat–cow flow

2 min · spine

On hands and knees, alternate rounding and arching the spine with the breath. Ten slow cycles.

Tadasana — mountain

1 min · balance

Stand tall, feet together, arms by the sides. Lengthen the crown, breathe slowly, feel yourself grounded.

Seated twist

1 min · each side

Cross-legged, hand on the opposite knee, exhale and rotate. Wrings out the spine after sitting.

Ankle & wrist circles

1 min · joints

Ten slow circles each ankle and wrist. Small movement that makes the forward fold and plank kind.

Legs up the wall

5 min · rest

Lie with the hips near a wall and legs vertical. Exhale slowly — the perfect way to close a session.

Take care. Skip inversions, deep backbends and the eight-limbed salute during pregnancy, or with high blood pressure, recent surgery or acute back, wrist or shoulder injury. Work with a qualified teacher in person when you can. This is education, not medical advice.
