The practice

How to practise

Simple, unhurried, repeatable. Five minutes is a complete practice.

01

Choose

Pick the centre that feels tight or tired — root when anxious, throat before speaking, heart when hurt.

02

Sit & see

Sit tall. Let the colour fill the screen. Breathe slowly in through the nose, out through the mouth.

03

Listen

Play the tone and set the timer. Imagine the colour and the sound gathering at that centre.

04

Release

When the bell rings, let it go. Notice what feels different before you open your eyes.

A note. Chakra work is a contemplative tradition, not a medical treatment. If you carry significant trauma, practise gently or with a teacher you trust.
